# Third Ear 0.1.2

- **One big Stop key while recording.** The line you tapped to start is the plan: it becomes a large "Stop → line" key under the timer, so you no longer look for it among the other lines. Pause is a full-width button below it with room in between. The other lines fold away under "Stop on another line", and Discard sits last, behind a confirmation.
- **Know when to speak.** A Bluetooth microphone takes a few seconds to come up. "Listening cue" plays a tone or says "Go ahead" once the microphone really records (always a tone on the phone's own microphone, so the clip doesn't end up in the recording). "After the stop" says what happens next ("Saved, transcribing.") or plays a tone, then a tone when it is delivered or fails. Each can be turned off. Turning off the old "Spoken cues" switch carries over as both off.
- **Leave White Noise groups.** Open a group under Lines → Groups and tap Leave group. The others see Third Ear leave, and only a new invite brings it back.
- **Next step after an invite.** A joined group without a line says so, with "Make a line" opening the editor already set to send to that group.
- **Back up the White Noise key.** Until the nsec of the current identity has been shown once, Lines and Setup remind you that only this phone has it, with a direct way to back it up.
