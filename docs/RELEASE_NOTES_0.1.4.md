# Third Ear 0.1.4

- **App shortcuts.** Long-press the Third Ear icon: the first four lines and "Stop recording". A line shortcut works like the widget: tap to record on that line, tap again to stop and send. Drag one to the home screen to keep it there. The menu follows your lines when you add, rename, reorder or delete them.
- **Bind a line to a button or an automation.** Launchers, button-mapping and automation apps (Key Mapper, Button Mapper, Tasker and others) list Third Ear's "Record on a line" shortcut. Pick any line, or "Stop recording", which stops and sends on the line the recording started with. Other apps cannot start a recording through these shortcuts: each one carries a key only your install knows.
- **A shortcut outlives its line** only as a notice: "This line no longer exists", instead of recording somewhere else. Pinned ones are greyed out.
