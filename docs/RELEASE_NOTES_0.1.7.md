# Third Ear 0.1.7

- **Record from the car screen without Google Play.** Third Ear now shows up in Android Auto as a media app, which Android Auto allows for apps from Zapstore once Unknown sources is on in its developer settings. The Record tab lists your lines: tap one and it records. Now Playing is the recording screen, with pause, ■ stop and save, and ✕ discard. To send the recording somewhere else, tap the back arrow while recording and pick "Stop → line". While a recording runs, the steering wheel's play/pause pauses it and "next" saves it. The Messages tab plays your recent White Noise messages.
- **"Play" never starts a recording.** Android Auto resumes the last media app when the car connects, and headphones send "play" too. Only a tap on a line starts a recording.
- **Why a media app:** since Android Auto 17.x, car apps built with Google's Car App Library run only when Google Play installed them. The grid version is still in the app, and Android Auto shows it where it is allowed. The website explains the setup: https://jooray.github.io/third-ear/#car
