# Third Ear 0.1.3

- **Talk in Messages, not only listen.** An open White Noise group has a text field and a big record key. The recording sends your voice, or only the transcript (a switch above the key). It works for every joined group, also one without a line on the Board, and a note still on its way shows above the field, with Retry when it was not delivered.
- **A real player for voice messages.** Each voice message shows its length and waveform. Tap or drag on the waveform to jump, pause, and change the speed from 1× to 2×. Voice messages you sent and older ones play too: they are downloaded when the conversation shows them.
- **Playback speed, per group.** Setup has a default speed for voice messages, and each group can have its own (Lines, Groups): an agent that already talks fast stays at 1×, people play at 1.5× or 2×. The speed also applies when messages play by themselves or from the notification.
- **Pick any folder for recordings.** The first recording asks where recordings go, and the folder you pick is the folder; a subfolder of Music no longer ends in "Wrong folder selected" ([#7](https://github.com/jooray/third-ear/issues/7)). Setup's Save recordings in works the same way. Installs that granted Music before keep working.
- **Tablets and wide screens.** Messages shows the groups and the open conversation side by side.
- **Smaller things.** Conversations sit at the bottom by the field and scroll to a new message, the keyboard no longer hides the header, times read like a chat app. In the car, a sender named like its group is said once, and when Android refuses the microphone (Third Ear not opened on the phone since a restart or an update) the car says so instead of "Starting the microphone" forever.
