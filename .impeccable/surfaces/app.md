## Scope

Whole Android app (Operate): Board, recording, Log (recordings, player, recycle bin), Lines (actions, White Noise groups), Setup, plus widget, tiles, notification reach. Power users first; hearing-aid and headset wearers must still read it at a glance. Website screenshots follow the build.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every action is a line on a switchboard: its own cord colour, a short line code, a printed designation strip and a lamp. Replies light the line they came in on. Refuses the category default of a round record button over a waveform with a recordings list.

OWN-WORLD: Bakelite-black face (dark) and designation-strip paper face (light), both flat, tonal steps and 1 dp rules, no shadows. Cord colours (red, green, blue, yellow, white, violet, orange, teal) are action identity, never decoration. Opal lamps: off, amber busy, white reply, red failed, always with a word. Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading, Saira Condensed caps for strips, codes and labels; tabular figures.

STORY: Power user sees input, every line's state and waiting replies without opening anything; taps a line to record, taps "Stop → line" to route; follows delivery on the last plate and in Log.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top: wordmark, search, menu. Pinned input bar (source, route, live level). "Lines" label, two-column field of line keys, each: code in cord colour cell, name 19 sp, lamp top right, designation strip at the bottom. Below: last-recording plate with pipeline state and actions. Bottom nav Board, Log, Lines, Setup; rail on expanded width. Recording: the started line becomes the one reversed plate, timer is the one giant figure.

FORM: The Jack Field, position 3 on the ordered list (switchboard designation strips), seed:9850a8edbbf25a7e. Raises: whole-cell density and line codes; one reversed plate per screen; relay-step motion (two-frame, no easing); one giant live figure.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
